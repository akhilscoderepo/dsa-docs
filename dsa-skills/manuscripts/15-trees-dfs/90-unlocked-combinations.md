## Unlocked Combinations

One combination now has all of its ingredients, and it receives a lesson and a ladder of its own. One more is recorded as deferred, with the later chapter that supplies the missing piece.

### Teach Now

The tree together with depth-first return state is the tenth lesson. The tree supplies subproblems that never overlap, and the depth-first visit supplies the moment when both branches have finished. What needs care is the split between what a node hands to its parent and what it only uses for itself. The ladder starts with a height paired with a count, continues through a diameter that records how often its best was reached, and then moves to a balance check with weights and a tolerance. It ends with a bent path sum that also reports where the best path turns. The false friend is returning the complete answer upward, which describes a chain that can fork.

### Deferred

A tree walked level by level needs a frontier that holds exactly one layer at a time, so that distance from the root is preserved. The tree part is ready, but the queue discipline, layer sizes, zigzag output and side views are the business of Chapter 16. No level-order problem is assigned here.

### Already Covered

Explicit stacks and their last-in, first-out order were taught in Chapter 11, and the push order argument in the iterative lesson relies on it directly. Index maps for constant-time position lookup come from Chapter 04. The reasoning with a loop invariant over references was practised in Chapter 14, and the habit of choosing a sentinel outside the valid range echoes the sentinel discussions of the binary search chapter.
