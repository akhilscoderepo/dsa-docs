## Unlocked Combinations

This chapter releases no teach-now combination. Every technique here works on a single array with a single pass or a small number of passes, and the earlier chapter contributes only habits, not a technique that would combine with them. Combining a technique with a table of counts, or with two moving boundaries, needs a prerequisite that is not yet taught.

### Deferred

Hash maps over arrays wait for Chapter 04, which provides lookup from a key to remembered state. The frequency arrays lesson already shows the counting idea over a small promised range, and Chapter 04 removes the range promise. Two pointers over arrays wait for Chapter 08, which provides movement of two boundaries. The read and write indices in stable compaction move in the same direction and are not the two-boundary pattern. Prefix sums, binary search and sorting have their own chapters. No exercise in this chapter depends on a technique from a later chapter.

### Already Covered

The contract sheet from Chapter 00 is used by every in-place lesson here, and the cost counting from Chapter 00 is used in every bottleneck section. Nothing else from earlier chapters is combined with the techniques in this chapter.
