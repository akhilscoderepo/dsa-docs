## Unlocked Combinations

No new teach-now combinations are released by this chapter, because it introduces habits of reading and counting and no standalone technique that could be combined with an earlier one. Chapter 00 is the first chapter, so there is no earlier technique to combine with.

### Deferred

Arrays with hash maps are deferred to Chapter 04, which supplies key-to-state lookup. Arrays with two pointers are deferred to Chapter 08, which supplies two-boundary movement. Prefix sums, binary search and sorting each have their own chapters, and the cost arguments from this chapter apply to them without change. Several exercises here point forward on purpose, such as the range-sum query exercise, which stops at deciding that a faster plan is needed and leaves the plan itself to Chapter 07. No exercise in this chapter assigns a problem whose solution needs a technique that has not been taught.

### Already Covered

Nothing earlier exists to link back to. Later chapters link back here for amortized cost, which the two-stack queue and the sliding window rely on, and for the contract sheet, which every in-place exercise in Chapter 01 uses.
