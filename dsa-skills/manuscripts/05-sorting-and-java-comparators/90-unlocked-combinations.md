## Unlocked Combinations

One combination is released by this chapter, because every prerequisite is now taught, and it has its own lesson with a four-step practice ladder. Another is deferred to a later owner, and a third is already covered by lessons that exist.

### Teach Now

Strings with maps and sorting is the final lesson of the chapter. Chapter 03 supplied traversal and character arrays, Chapter 04 supplied maps that hold a list of members under each key, and the lessons here supplied the sorted canonical form. Together they answer questions in which two strings belong together exactly when their characters agree with repeats counted. The ladder covers valid anagrams, grouping anagrams, ordering characters by frequency, and the weaker relation of close strings. Its false friend is a frequency-array signature offered as the reason the technique works.

### Deferred

Sorting with two pointers is deferred to Chapter 08. What is missing is a safe rule for moving the pointers and a policy for duplicate values, and Chapter 08 owns both. This chapter mentions in passing that squares of a sorted array have a linear solution, and it assigns no exercise that needs pointers. Intervals, greedy choices, binary search over sorted input, and quickselect are also deferred, to Chapters 10, 20, 06 and 32 in that order, and none of their problems appear here.

### Already Covered

The relation between arrays and sorting is covered by the sort-and-sweep and sort-then-scan lessons, which treat a sorted array as a sequence that a scan can read from left to right. The run-based reading of equal values is covered by the sort-and-deduplicate lesson, which returns to the duplicate detection of Chapter 01 with a different contract.
