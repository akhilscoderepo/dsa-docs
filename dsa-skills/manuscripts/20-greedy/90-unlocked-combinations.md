## Unlocked Combinations

A sorted scan that never changes its mind passes every sample and fails one hidden input. This chapter releases one pairing in full, lists three that its prerequisites also allow, and names one that waits for a later chapter.

### Pairing Taught In This Chapter

Ordering and the exchange argument form the lesson Sort Then Commit Greedily. The order supplies the front item, and the argument supplies the reason to commit to it. The lesson adds one idea. The sort key is the number that the exchange step compares, and for a string cut into parts that number is the last position of each letter. Its exercises cover the satisfied children of a cookie match, a gap between kept intervals, half-open balloons and the lengths of the parts of a stream.

### Pairings That The Prerequisites Also Allow

Three more pairings are open, and each needs its own ladder in a later pass over this chapter. A greedy rule with a heap keeps the best option seen so far, as with climbing buildings and refueling stops. A greedy rule with a monotonic stack removes a worse earlier digit while a removal budget remains. A greedy rule with two pointers pairs the lightest and the heaviest item when the sum fits. The chapter assigns none of their problems, so no exercise here depends on them.

### One Pairing Waits For A Later Chapter

Some problems look greedy but compare reusable future states, and a local rule fails on them. Telling them apart needs the state framework of Chapters 26 to 29, and those chapters own the problems of that pairing.
